오늘은 핵시계 이야기예요

5문장 B1 위주 B2 1문장 약 3분
STEP 1

오늘의 지문

Most of us check the time on our phones, but the most exact clocks in the world work in a very different way.
Earlier this month, scientists in Vienna reported that they had built a new kind of clock that uses the center of an atom, called the nucleus.
A laser shines on a special crystal that was made by the team, and the clock corrects the laser by itself when it goes off track.
The clock, which ran for more than 24 hours without any help, would lose only about one second in thirty million years.B2
It is not yet as accurate as the best atomic clocks, but the researchers plan to improve it with stronger lasers and better crystals.

4번 문장 끝의 one second in thirty million years를 놓치지 마세요. thirty million은 30,000,000, 즉 3천만이에요. 같은 문장의 more than 24 hours도 '24시간 넘게'예요.

수동태의 행위자 (by)
