STEP 1

오늘의 지문

On the first Saturday of every month, a small community hall turns into a repair café for about four hours.
People arrive with broken lamps, torn jackets and old radios, and they sit down next to volunteers who know how to fix them.
The repairs are free, but visitors are asked to watch and help, so they can learn to do it themselves next time.
Last month, a teenager's headphones were fixed by a retired electrician in less than twenty minutes.
The organizers say that around seventy items are repaired each month, and that most of them would otherwise end up in the trash.
For many regular visitors, the café, which started as a simple way to reduce waste, has become a place where neighbors who once passed each other in silence now stop to talk.B2
