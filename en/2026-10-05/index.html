STEP 1

오늘의 지문

Three scientists won the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for a technique that uses light to control individual brain cells.
Their method, called optogenetics, works by inserting light-sensitive proteins found in algae directly into nerve cells.
When a beam of light shines on these modified cells, the proteins open tiny channels and allow electrical signals to pass in or out.
This lets researchers switch a single nerve cell on or off with great precision, something that was simply not possible with earlier tools.
Optogenetics is now used in laboratories around the world to study how networks of brain cells drive behaviour, memory, and the feeling of pain.B2

STEP 3

오늘의 체크 포인트

lets + 사람 + 동사원형

4번의 This lets researchers switch는 'lets + 사람 + 동사원형' 구조예요. This lets me go(이게 나를 가게 해줘)와 같은 패턴이에요. allows + 사람 + to V와 다르게 to 없이 쓰여요.

자극하다 · simulate(시뮬레이션하다)와 혼동 주의

illuminate
빛을 비추다, 밝히다
Scientists illuminate the target cells with a thin fibre-optic cable.
neuron
신경 세포, 뉴런

manipulate
조작하다, 다루다

therapeutic

breakthrough

The Nobel committee called optogenetics a true breakthrough in brain research.
ion channel

The protein acts as an ion channel, opening a gate only when light hits it.
